Our address:

School of Mathematics, University of Bristol, University Walk, Bristol BS8 1TW. Google map, official directions page. Entering the building via the main entrance take the elevator to the third floor, or walk up one floor then follow the yellow "Portacabins" signs, but do not exit the building, to reach further stairs to the third floor.
