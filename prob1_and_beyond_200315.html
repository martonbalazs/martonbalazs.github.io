Elementary probability notes

The purpose of this page is to collect material for a solid foundation in elementary probability. It consists of my University of Bristol Probability 1 Unit lecture slides, plus additional material that could not be included due to the lack of prerequisites or time.

  • The lecture slides
     
  • Combinations with repetitions
     
  • Measure theoretic definition of probability; continuity of probability; proper definition of random variables
     
  • Inclusion-exclusion for n events
     
  • Bernoulli LLN; Weierstrass approximation theorem
     
  • Poisson process
     
  • Negative Binomial, Hypergeometric distributions
     
  • The Normal density is normed
     
  • Stirling's formula, DeMoivre-Laplace local and global Central Limit Theorem
     
  • Joint continuous distributions; joint distribution transformations; Multivariate Normal; covariance matrix; proof of the continuous convolution formula
     
  • Continuous conditional distributions
     
  • Steiner's theorem and least squares
     
  • Joint moment generating functions
     
  • A few nice probability problems
